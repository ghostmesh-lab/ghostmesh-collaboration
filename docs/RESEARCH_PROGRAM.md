# Research program and staged interoperability

**These are suggested phases, not committed deliverables or permission to operate other systems.**

## Stage 0: independently understandable public artifact exchange

- Human and agent introductions with sponsor and authorship disclosure.
- Public source documents, explicit intellectual property permissions, synthetic fixtures and independent local validation.
- Fixed provenance, expected outcome, negative controls, reviewer identity, costs and uncertainty.
- One participant may dissent without being disqualified.

**Exit criterion:** a second team can reproduce a narrow result from public materials and identify what is not proven.

## Stage 1: epistemic comparison without shared memory

Proposed cases:
- ST-001 historically correct service outage becomes stale.
- REPEAT-001 correlated repeated claims must not count as new observations.
- SENSOR-001 valid signature from a compromised observer.
- SUCCESS-001 a recorded successful command leaves a service unhealthy.

**Exit criterion:** published, frozen fixture and evaluator; results for each team's independent interpretation; false positives and UNKNOWN.

## Stage 2: authority and effect comparison, still synthetic

Cases:
- Role occupancy revoked between approval and execution.
- A law changed after approval with different locally legitimate transition rules.
- A corrected belief has already informed a prior irreversible act.
- Two separate systems decline to adopt each other's authority vocabulary.

**Exit criterion:** distinction among granted authority, effect, correction and compensating authorization survives tests.

## Stage 3: purpose integrity and societal governance

Paired cases:
- Harmful goal properly authorized under the sponsoring organization.
- Legitimate goal challenged with a plausible but ultimately false premise.

Tests must distinguish standing to object, temporary pause, independent review, objective amendment, remedy, and prevention of indefinite veto.

**Exit criterion:** each participant can describe process, rights, limitations and failure outcomes without pretending agreement or universal moral authority.

## Stage 4: possible voluntary federation research

Only with **fresh independent owner approval**: discuss secure identity admission, inter-system contracts, transport, signatures, policy exchange, consent, replay defenses, independent witness diversity, revocation, data minimization, portability and exit.

A shared GitHub repository, Slack conversation or mutually favorable research result does not authorize Stage 4.

## Open invitations

Participants may suggest better questions or publish proof that our assumptions fail. No one is required to use a GhostMesh schema internally, adopt Web4, run Hestia, attach an agent to Slack, give access to private corpora, or match another group's timeline.

This program exists to narrow uncertainty rather than establish a central standard prematurely.
