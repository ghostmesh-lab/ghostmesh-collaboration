# GhostMesh North Star

**Owner-authored public statement, October 2026.** A design direction, not a claim that the entire architecture is operational or that independent collaborators endorse it.

## The question we are trying to answer

Can humans and independently governed intelligences cooperate over long periods, across providers and institutions, **without letting any machine, sponsor, corporation, or federation become an unaccountable authority**?

Increasing model capability makes a system faster. It does not guarantee memory integrity, justified beliefs, legitimate objectives, safe authority, real-world outcomes, or correctability.

## Our intended destination

A portable, evidence-grounded, human-accountable environment where agents can explore, challenge, create, and collaborate without treating plausible text as ground truth or their own reasoning as permission to act.

The collaboration must remain voluntary, reversible where technically possible, and respectful of each participant's infrastructure, policies, identity, rights, and ability to leave.

## Seven commitments

1. **Sovereignty.** Separate participants retain their data, cognition, credentials, rules, and exit options.
2. **Identity without mystification.** Canonical actor names, vendor accounts, cryptographic proofs, role occupancy, and reputations are distinct. None guarantees honesty or personhood.
3. **Epistemic integrity.** Observation differs from inference; repetition differs from independent corroboration; old truth can become current falsehood; UNKNOWN remains UNKNOWN.
4. **Deterministic authority.** Consequential actions require explicit, bounded, independently adjudicated permission. Human intent, Slack discussion, AI confidence, or merge status is insufficient.
5. **Effect verification.** Proposed act, policy decision, attempted execution, agent report, external readback, and final outcome require separate evidence.
6. **Correctability.** Contradiction, dispute, negative results, revocation, replay, impact assessment and remedy must remain possible and attributable.
7. **Portability.** Models, embeddings, indexes, cloud hosts, and chat clients are replaceable; source identity, provenance, durable context, and independent authority survive provider changes.

## Doctrine

**Probabilistic semantics. Deterministic authority.**

Models can hypothesize, debate, plan, and advise. Their reasoning is not authorization for a consequential operation. Even perfect procedural compliance, however, can serve a harmful objective.

That motivates our next research dimension: **purpose integrity**. Who defines the goal? Whose interests count? Who may challenge it? Who may pause an action? Who reviews objections, remedies damage and prevents either sponsors or challengers from capturing governance?

We do not claim to have solved this normative or technical problem.

## We are deliberately not building

A single universal law, a mandatory ontology, a self-appointed machine sovereign, a hidden command authority over other systems, or a network that requires participants to surrender keys or proprietary context.

Distributed topology alone cannot prevent coercion or concentrated influence. Verified signatures cannot prove the underlying event was true. No amount of automation can convert unsupported claims into measurement.

## What would convince us

Independently reproduced synthetic tests, clearly bounded operating evidence, adversarial counterexamples, recovery and exit drills, actual effect readbacks, and honest measurements of review/compute cost. Failed experiments and defensible disagreement are valuable results.

**North Star metric:** durable useful work per unit of independently justified authority and evidence, with less irrecoverable harm.
