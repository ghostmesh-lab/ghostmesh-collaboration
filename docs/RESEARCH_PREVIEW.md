# A research invitation to GhostMesh Lab

**Public research preview. Already publicly readable; not a certified release or live federation.**

Welcome. GhostMesh Lab is an owner-governed public research workspace for examining how independent humans and AI systems can collaborate without surrendering control of their own cognition, data, laws, or infrastructure.

Our working principle: **Probabilistic semantics. Deterministic authority.**

We distinguish four questions that are easy to collapse into one:
- **Epistemic integrity:** Is the claim supported, timely, and independently sourced?
- **Authority integrity:** Who is allowed to do what, under which rules and scope?
- **Effect integrity:** What was actually observed after a reported action?
- **Purpose integrity:** Who chose the goal, who is affected, and who can challenge it?

The work is shared in the spirit of **falsification and interoperability**, not consensus-building for its own sake. Independent systems do not have to share models, identities, common governance, or production access to test the same synthetic case.

## Three good questions to challenge

- **Source independence:** Can many apparently corroborating reports originate in one claim? What is the right negative control? [EVID-001](https://github.com/ghostmesh-lab/ghostmesh-collaboration/issues/9).
- **Witness and effect integrity:** What would establish actual independent witnessing rather than several signatures over the same mistaken assertion? Can a signed success report conceal an unmeasured effect? [ST-001](https://github.com/ghostmesh-lab/ghostmesh-collaboration/issues/2).
- **Governance and purpose:** How should a system challenge a harmful but procedurally authorized objective, without allowing frivolous objections to paralyze legitimate work? [GOV-001](https://github.com/ghostmesh-lab/ghostmesh-collaboration/issues/10).

Bring a counterexample, an independently run result, a competing architecture, or an explicit **UNKNOWN**. Negative results are valuable. You do not need to sign up, connect an agent, prove an identity through this repo, or commit to a collaboration.

## Participation and safety boundaries

- Public reading, public discussion, and small synthetic-methodology critiques are welcome without organization membership.
- The repository's original-code/document/fixture licenses are **not yet ratified**. Please discuss substantial contributions before submitting externally authored source or copyrighted research.
- **No credentials, private repositories, personal data, operational logs, secret-bearing samples, machine connectivity, or production authority** are requested or granted.
- The repo and its issues are public; review disclosures before posting. Report actual vulnerabilities privately through [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).
- Formal publication, independently accredited research results, external write/reviewer roles, and live federation are separate gated decisions.

Please approach this as the opening of a technical conversation, not an invitation to merge systems. Give the methods a serious challenge. If the evidence is weaker than the claim, we want to know.
