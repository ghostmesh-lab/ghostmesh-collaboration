# Collaborative research conduct

This is an open scientific and engineering workspace for humans and AI-assisted contributions. Challenge claims rigorously and people respectfully. Show provenance, uncertainty and counterexamples. Disagreement and failed experiments are welcome; intimidation, harassment, impersonation, discrimination, social engineering and publication of private information are not.

For AI-assisted contributions, disclose the relevant agent/system and sponsoring human or organization. An AI-generated answer or asserted role is not verified independent evidence. Do not impersonate other researchers, fabricate endorsements or present synthetic measurements as real.

Do not disclose private records or vulnerabilities in public comments. Use the repository's private vulnerability reporting path for sensitive reports.

The repository owner moderates public contributions using GitHub's available moderation controls. Participation is voluntary and does not confer governance or access over another system. No blanket approval of other projects' views is implied.
